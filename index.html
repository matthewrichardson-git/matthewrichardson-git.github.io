Monte Carlo Option Pricing Simulator

Why I built this

I had a finance background but no idea how options were actually priced under the hood. I wanted to combine what I already knew with coding, so I built this from scratch. I simulated 1000 possible stock price futures using Geometric Brownian Motion, priced European call and put options, and validated the results against the Black-Scholes analytical formula. The two prices come out within cents of each other, which means the simulation works.
